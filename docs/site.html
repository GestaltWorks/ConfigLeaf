The stack

Zetaris, Meterless and NVIDIA Nemotron

Zetaris

Every query of a live pass runs through Lightning SQL over the DRIFT datasource, with the drift_usl semantic layer and its DQ rules over it.

Meterless

The World Model holds the observations and the fingerprints FOUND-BUT-DIFFERENT compares, H-MEM holds the technician's lessons, and Nightshift serves Nemotron and signs a receipt for every call.

NVIDIA Nemotron

3.5 Lightning selects the checks and 3 Ultra writes the SELECT that refutes a false claim, each verdict counted through the receipt gate.

Decide

Each finding under the MSP's policy

ROLLBACK

A drift from the approved value under a locked or rollback rule, with a valid vault copy of that value.

FIX, ESCALATE or ADOPT

The same drift with no valid copy and production affected, as on_no_copy says; ESCALATE when production is unknown.

HOLD

The same drift with production answering, on_drift hold, or a finding about a setting's relation to another setting; the expiry runs hold_max_hours from the first HOLD on that live value, and past it the finding is ESCALATE.

ADOPT

on_drift adopt, or the technician's exception in force; with no rule, ADOPT when every check holds and ESCALATE otherwise.
